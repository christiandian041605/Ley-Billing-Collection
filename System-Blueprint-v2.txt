SYSTEM OVERVIEW:
===============
Web-based billing and collection system tailored for government and private sales.
KEY REVISION: Separate workflows for Private and Government sales.
- Private: Sales tracked via Deliveries (DR V). No Invoice step.
- Government: Sales tracked via Charge Invoices linked to Deliveries (DR Government).

Main Flow 1 (Private): Customer → Delivery (DR V) → Payment → Receipt → Report
Main Flow 2 (Government): Customer → Delivery (DR Govt) → Charge Invoice → Payment → Receipt → Report
