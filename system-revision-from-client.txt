================================================================================
LEY GOVERNMENT BILLING AND COLLECTION SYSTEM - REVISED BLUEPRINT (Dec 2025)
================================================================================

SYSTEM OVERVIEW:
===============
Web-based billing and collection system tailored for government and private sales.
KEY CHANGE: Private sales are recorded via DR V and skip the Charge Invoice step.
KEY CHANGE: Charge Invoices are now exclusively for Government sales.

Revised Main Flow:
1. Private: Customer → DR V (Delivery) → Payment → Receipt → Report
2. Government: Customer → DR Government (Delivery) → Charge Invoice → Payment → Receipt → Report

3. DELIVERY MANAGEMENT (Now a Key Sales Document for Private)
=============================================================
MODULE PATH: /deliveries/
FUNCTION:
- Track all product/service deliveries.
- **DR V** (Delivery Receipt - Vendor): Serves as the **primary sales document** for **Private/Business** customers.
- **DR Government**: Serves as the supporting document for **Government** Charge Invoices.

KEY OPERATION REVISION:
- **ACTION:** Add `payment_status` to track private sales payment state.

DATABASE TABLE REVISION:
- tbl_deliveries
  * id (PK)
  * delivery_no
  * customer_id (FK → tbl_customers)
  * delivery_type
  * total_amount
  * **payment_status (ENUM: Unpaid, Partially Paid, Paid)** <- NEW FIELD
  * ...

---

4. CHARGE INVOICES (Government Exclusive - Items Removed)
=========================================================
MODULE PATH: /charge_invoices/
FUNCTION:
- **Exclusively** create invoices for services rendered to **Government** customers.
- Calculate withholding tax (5% and 1%).
- Track invoice payment status.

BUSINESS RULE REVISION:
- Charge Invoices MUST link to **DR Government** deliveries **only**.
- **The invoice will no longer have line items.**

DATA FLOW REVISION:
- The **Total Amount** for the Invoice is now derived directly from the **Total Amount** of the linked **DR Government** delivery record(s).

DATABASE TABLES REVISIONS:
- **tbl_charge_invoices** (Structural Change): The `total_amount` is now derived from linked `tbl_deliveries`.
  * ... (No change to columns)
- **tbl_charge_invoice_items** (ELIMINATED):
  * **ACTION:** This table is **REMOVED** from the system blueprint.

- **tbl_invoice_deliveries** (Junction table - No change):
  * Links `charge_invoice_id` to `delivery_id` (DR Government only).

---

5. PAYMENTS & OFFICIAL RECEIPTS (Dual Linkage to Delivery OR Invoice)
=====================================================================
MODULE PATH: /payments/
FUNCTION:
- Process customer payments.
- Generate Official Receipts (OR).
- Allocate payments to either a **Delivery** (Private) or an **Invoice** (Government).

PAYMENT WORKFLOW REVISIONS:

A. PRIVATE/BUSINESS PAYMENTS:
  * **Linkage:** Payment must link to the **DR V** (Delivery record).
  * **Lookup:** System searches by Customer Name, then lists their unpaid **DR V** records for payment application.

B. GOVERNMENT PAYMENTS:
  * **Linkage:** Payment must link to the **Charge Sales Invoice**.
  * **Lookup:** System searches by Customer Name, then lists their unpaid **Charge Invoices** for payment application.

DATABASE TABLES REVISION (New Junction Table for Private Sales):
- **tbl_official_receipts** (No change to columns)
- **tbl_or_invoice** (Retained for Government Linkage):
  * Links `or_id` to `charge_invoice_id` (Government only).

- **tbl_or_delivery** (NEW Junction Table for Private Linkage) <- NEW TABLE
  * id (PK)
  * **or_id (FK → tbl_official_receipts)**
  * **delivery_id (FK → tbl_deliveries)** <- Links OR to the DR V
  * applied_amount (DECIMAL 15,2) - Amount allocated to this DR V

- tbl_payment_history (No change to columns)
- tbl_banks (No change to columns)

---

6. REPORTS & STATEMENT OF ACCOUNTS (Automated Status)
======================================================
MODULE PATH: /reports/
FUNCTION:
- Generate Statement of Accounts (SOA).

KEY OPERATION REVISIONS:
- **Report Generation Fix:** The report query must be fixed to correctly retrieve and display **all** paid and unpaid records (Invoices and Deliveries) based on filter criteria.
- **Automated Status:** The SOA report must **automatically display** the status (Paid, Partially Paid, Unpaid) for each transaction item.
  * **Status Source:**
    * For **Government** transactions: Check `payment_status` in **tbl_charge_invoices**.
    * For **Private** transactions: Check **NEW `payment_status`** in **tbl_deliveries**.

REPORT INCLUDES (No Change):
- Customer Information, Transaction Details, Financial Summary.
